focus: follow-on directions after IWAI 2026 acceptance (Poster + Spotlight) of "Expected Free Energy as Belief-Dependent Utility for rho-POMDPs"

Grounding Context

Codebase context. The rho_aif package holds a full agent grid (EFE, Planning, Planning+IG, Myopic, Thompson, POMCP, MCTS-EFE, IDS, PyMDP, domain tree-search agents) over exact discrete Bayesian beliefs, eleven benchmark instances from Tiger through RockSample to Structural Inspection (up to 65,536 states), 18 reproduction scripts, and 251 passing tests. The accepted paper proves EFE equals a rho-POMDP with expected information gain at w=1 for observe-then-commit POMDPs (Prop 1), gives near-optimality thresholds w_lo and w_hi for two-state problems (Prop 2), and extends the equivalence to factored observation POMDPs (Prop 3).

Stated limitations. Exact search is O(K|O|^H), capping horizons at 2 to 3. Everything is discrete with a known model. The w=1 weight is not reward-scale invariant, being exact only under log scoring rules (Bernardo 1979). The equivalence breaks when observation actions alter the hidden state. SARSOP and POMCPOW baselines were deferred as future work in the camera-ready replies.

External landscape. The result sits in a thin seam between the Technion rho-POMDP group (Benchetrit et al. 2025 built an anytime continuous-space rho-POMCPOW) and active inference theory. Sequential Bayesian experimental design has an amortization toolkit (Deep Adaptive Design, Foster et al. 2021). LLM agents that gather information (BED-LLM, AutoDiscovery, InfoReasoner) all weight information gain heuristically. A fresh-context verifier spot-checked the load-bearing citations and prior art during filtering.

Topic Axes

  • theory — extending the formal results: state-changing observations, drifting states, scale-aware calibration of w, deeper near-optimality characterization
  • scaling-solvers — continuous spaces, rho-POMCPOW, progressive-widening MCTS-EFE, SARSOP/POMCPOW baselines, deep-horizon EFE
  • learned-models — dropping the known-model assumption: model learning under EFE, amortized/deep EFE, misspecification robustness
  • applications-benchmarks — domain-realistic uses: inspection, medical screening, robotics active sensing, benchmark suites
  • new-audiences — carrying the calibrated-information-weight result to LLM agents, experimental design, bandits, RL exploration

The Price of Information: w as a Shadow Price on a Sensing Budget

Description Replace the scale-dependent weight w with an operational sensing budget (number of tests, total observation cost, time on task) and derive the effective w as the Lagrange multiplier of the budgeted rho-POMDP, computable by bisection with existing solvers. Rational inattention in economics (Sims 2003, Matejka and McKay 2015) prices information exactly this way, and its entropy cost is the same log-scoring structure that makes w=1 exact in Prop 1. Pair the static duality with an online dual-descent rule that adjusts w during planning to hit a target information level, the same mechanism SAC-v2 used to eliminate its entropy-temperature hyperparameter. Prop 2's thresholds become corollaries of the duality statement.
Basis direct: the grounding lists "w=1 not reward-scale-invariant" as the paper's central conceded limitation and "success-tuned w* (20-200) beats w=1 on accuracy at reward cost" as the unexplained anomaly. external: Matejka and McKay (2015, AER) show the information price is the shadow cost of an attention constraint; Haarnoja et al. (2018) auto-tune the structurally identical entropy temperature by dual descent. Verifier caution: no universal closed form w*(R, gamma, |S|, H) follows without extra assumptions, and EFE-to-resource-rationality duality has prior art, so the contribution must be the budgeted rho-POMDP formulation and its empirical validation, not the duality observation itself.
Rationale This converts the paper's most awkward limitation, an uninterpretable scale-dependent dial, into its next theorem, and the reward-scaling, Pareto, and transfer scripts already in the repo are the empirical apparatus. It also reframes the reviewers' strongest objection as the motivation for the sequel.
Downsides Prior work on resource-rational and bounded-rational duality must be positioned against carefully or reviewers will call it a restatement. The clean results may hold only for the same restricted problem classes as Prop 2.

Active Inference Under Destructive Sensing

Description Extend Propositions 1 and 3 to observation actions that alter the hidden state (destructive testing, biopsy, invasive probing). The paper explicitly flags this as the case where the equivalence breaks. The move is to decompose the value of a state-changing observation into information gained minus future information foreclosed, drawing on quasi-option value from environmental economics (Arrow and Fisher 1974) as the framing, then derive the corrected Bellman operator and characterize when naive EFE over-tests or under-tests. A destructive variant of the existing Structural Inspection environment is the natural testbed.
Basis direct: the paper's Limitations section names "state-changing observations (destructive testing, full Delta_T)" as explicit future work, and the grounding notes "equivalence breaks when observation actions alter hidden state." external: Arrow and Fisher (1974, QJE) established quasi-option value for irreversible acts that both yield information and foreclose future information, with a known sign of bias. The verifier confirmed the analogy is framing, not a ready-made theorem, so the Bellman correction is the genuine contribution.
Rationale This is the most-flagged open problem in the accepted paper, it targets exactly the settings the applications story is built on (NDT and medical screening are dominated by costly or destructive probes), and it is a self-contained theory-plus-experiments paper achievable on the existing discrete infrastructure. The verifier rated it sound with no prior-art collision.
Downsides The corrected operator may not admit clean closed-form thresholds like Prop 2, and the result could land as incremental if the correction term is small in practice. Requires building a destructive-inspection environment variant.

The Inspection Benchmark, Released

Description Package the Structural Inspection environment plus the observe-then-commit suite as a standalone pip-installable benchmark for information-gathering planning, with documented baselines (all existing agents), standardized metrics, and reproducible configs. Two twists lift it above a code dump. First, score terminal beliefs with proper scoring rules alongside reward, since in screening and NDT the deliverable is a calibrated posterior, and under log scoring the w=1 agent is provably the right reporter. Second, emit a per-test value-of-information record per episode, giving the audit-trail artifact applied communities ask for. This forces the deferred SARSOP comparison into existence as a baseline PR rather than a paper burden.
Basis direct: "Structural Inspection benchmark (domain-realistic NDT/medical)" and the 18-script, 251-test infrastructure exist today; the camera-ready replies promise public code anyway. reasoned: no standard open benchmark exists for inspection and screening planning the way MuJoCo exists for control; benchmark and dataset papers have outsized citation impact relative to effort, and the marginal work over the promised code release is curation. Verifier rated it sound, noting value depends on genuinely difficult baselines.
Rationale Cheapest idea on the list with the longest tail. It compounds: every later paper (destructive sensing, continuous bridge, LLM calibration) gets its evaluation suite for free, and external users create the citations and collaborations that a solo researcher otherwise has to manufacture.
Downsides Benchmarks need maintenance and adoption work (docs, issues, CI) that competes with research time. A NeurIPS Datasets and Benchmarks submission needs more baselines than currently exist.

A Principled w for LLM Question-Askers

Description LLM agents that gather information before acting (BED-LLM, AutoDiscovery, InfoReasoner) weight information gain against task progress heuristically. Export the calibration machinery: given a task with a defined utility and per-question cost, compute the near-optimal weight band from Prop 2 style thresholds and show on a twenty-questions style benchmark with known ground-truth posteriors that heuristic weights provably under- or over-explore where the calibrated weight does not. The verifier's condition is the design spec: the demonstration must define the task utility explicitly and beat pure expected-information-gain maximization, not just a strawman.
Basis direct: the external grounding names "BED-LLM, AutoDiscovery, InfoReasoner pick information-gain weights heuristically, none has a principled w" and flags LLM-agent exploration as a top venue conversation. reasoned: the theory needs only a belief, an information measure, and a reward scale, none of which requires the agent to be a POMDP solver; the discrete testbed generates the ground-truth posteriors LLM evaluations usually lack.
Rationale Highest-traffic audience reachable with the least new theory. A four-page workshop paper at an agents venue could land within months, and it repositions the whole research program from "active inference internal affairs" to "calibration tool for the busiest agent question of 2026."
Downsides LLM beliefs are not exact Bayesian posteriors, so the transfer involves an approximation step reviewers will probe. Fast-moving field with real scoop risk. Success depends on the calibrated weight actually beating pure EIG on the chosen tasks, which is unverified.

The Continuous Bridge: EFE Meets rho-POMCPOW

Description Benchetrit et al. (2025) built an anytime continuous-space rho-POMCPOW with incremental entropy estimators but never made the EFE connection. Rebuild Structural Inspection with continuous defect severity, run EFE-as-rho inside their solver class, and test whether the Prop 1 identity and the Prop 2 near-optimality bands survive particle beliefs and entropy estimation. Run SARSOP on the discrete suite in the same paper to retire the deferred-baselines debt honestly. A differentiating algorithmic piece: govern progressive widening by marginal information gain per expansion rather than the standard polynomial schedule.
Basis direct: "discrete-only" is a stated limitation and SARSOP/POMCPOW baselines were promised as future work in the camera-ready replies. external: Benchetrit et al., arXiv 2502.02549, verified real by the filtering pass, which also corrected the framing: continuous rho-POMCPOW already exists with POMCPOW comparisons, so the contribution here is the empirical EFE bridge and the widening rule, not a new solver.
Rationale Whoever runs this experiment first owns the continuous half of the seam between the two communities. It converts the accepted paper's two acknowledged empirical gaps into the sequel's headline table.
Downsides Heaviest engineering lift on the list (particle filters, entropy estimators, a Julia or reimplemented solver stack). The verifier judged the current shape closer to an application paper than a solver contribution, so novelty rests on the widening rule and the equivalence-survival analysis.

When Is Myopic Enough? Adaptive-Submodularity Certificates for Greedy EFE

Description Attack the H=2-3 horizon ceiling from below: instead of scaling search deeper, prove when depth is unnecessary. In Prop 3's factored-observation setting, information gain is a candidate for adaptive submodularity (Golovin and Krause 2011), which would give greedy EFE a constant-factor guarantee with no lookahead. The verifier's caution defines the research question: EFE's reward term, observation noise, and transitions can violate the submodularity conditions, so the deliverable is a certificate identifying which environment families qualify, with the testbed's known "EFE needs H at least 2" cases as the boundary-mapping counterexamples.
Basis direct: "exact search O(K*|O|^H) limits H=2-3" is the paper's main computational wall, and the Myopic and Planning agent pairs needed to test any claim already exist. external: Golovin and Krause (JAIR 2011) provide the greedy guarantee framework for adaptive stochastic optimization. reasoned: a sufficiency certificate telling practitioners when they can skip planning entirely is more useful than a solver speedup, and negative results (which families fail, and why) are publishable in this framing.
Rationale It would dissolve rather than mitigate the scaling limitation for a characterized problem class, and it produces theory that stands regardless of which way the certificates fall.
Downsides Real risk the conditions fail almost everywhere interesting, leaving a mostly negative paper. Proof work is nontrivial and further from the existing codebase than the other candidates.
