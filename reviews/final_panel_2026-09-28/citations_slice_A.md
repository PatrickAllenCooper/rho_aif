## Minor notes (no field is wrong)

- `araya2010`: the NeurIPS metadata lists the first author as "Araya, Mauricio". The bib's
  "Araya-L\'{o}pez, M." is the author's full surname as printed on the paper and used in his other
  publications, so this is not an error. The bib omits the NeurIPS volume (23), which is optional.
- `champion2024` and `dacosta2020b`: the citation keys carry 2024 and 2020, while the printed years
  (2026 and 2023) are the correct published years. Labels only, nothing printed is wrong.
- `devries2025`: the bib gives three authors plus "and others". The arXiv record has 16 authors
  (de Vries, Nuijten, van de Laar, Kouw, Adamiat, Nisslbeck, Lukashchuk, Nguyen, Hidalgo Araya,
  Tresor, Jenneskens, Nikoloska, Ganapathy Subramanian, van Erp, Bagaev, Podusenko). The three listed
  are correct and in order. The truncation is a deliberate et al., not a wrong author list.
- `benchetrit2025`: still an arXiv preprint (v1, "Submitted to IJCAI 2025"). I found no published
  version, so the arXiv entry is the correct form.
- `duff2002`: at least one later citing paper dates the dissertation to 2003, probably from the
  ProQuest record. The author's own May 2002 announcement and the Mathematics Genealogy Project both
  give 2002, so the bib's year stands.
- `altman1999`: the only DOI is for the 2021 CRC reprint. The bib correctly cites the 1999 original.
- `cooper2026iwai`: the tex says "accepted as a poster and spotlight presentation at the 7th
  International Workshop on Active Inference". The arXiv comment confirms the spotlight and the CCIS
  venue. "Poster" and "7th" come from the project record, not from an external primary source.
  The ordinal matches IWAI's numbering (first edition 2020).

## Citation-context check (misattributions)

No clear misattributions. Each citing sentence was compared with the source:

- `altman1999` (L155, L419, L509, L718): Lagrangian dualization of expected-cost constraints,
  randomized optimal constrained policies, and strong duality for finite constrained MDPs are all
  standard results in Altman's book.
- `araya2010` (L81, L125, L127 x2, L197 x2, L388): belief-dependent reward, the action-dependent
  rho(b,a) with an additive expected-reward component, convex rho giving a convex value function,
  and PWLC approximation of convex rho are all in the paper.
- `bellemare2016`, `burda2019` (L152): count-based exploration and RND bonuses, both with a tunable
  bonus scale. Correct.
- `bernardo1979` (L87, L134, L225, L1077, L1842): expected information as expected utility under a
  logarithmic (proper local) scoring rule. Matches the Project Euclid abstract.
- `benchetrit2025` (L127, L1052): rho-POMCPOW, an anytime incremental rho-POMDP solver for
  continuous spaces built on POMCPOW, which uses progressive widening. The full text discusses
  progressive widening and DPW-based baselines (PFT-DPW), so the L1052 pointer is supported.
- `blum1954` (L497, L1842): almost-sure convergence of stochastic approximation (Robbins-Monro).
  Correct.
- `boutilier2002` (L129): preference elicitation as a POMDP over utility functions, with sequential
  rather than myopic value of information. Matches the AAAI abstract and paper.
- `champion2024` (L141, L218): the "unification problem" and two candidate root definitions of EFE
  match the Crossref abstract, which analyzes "two approaches to defining expected free energy".
- `cooper2026iwai` (L111): self-citation to the accepted workshop version. Consistent.
- `dacosta2020` (L139): the policy posterior as a softmax of negative EFE is in the synthesis paper.
- `dacosta2020b` (L146, L218, L222, L1842): Bellman optimality of sophisticated inference in the
  zero-temperature limit. Correct.
- `devries2025` (L141, L218): EFE planning as variational inference with preference and epistemic
  priors (abstract). "Leaves message-passing implementations to future work" is supported by the
  full text: "We intend to describe simulations of this process in forthcoming publications", and a
  discussion section framed as "a promising direction for future research".
- `duff2002`, `guez2013`, `ghavamzadeh2015` (L152): Bayes-adaptive MDPs and Bayesian RL under model
  uncertainty. Correct.
- `fehr2018` (L127 x2, L197): Lipschitz-continuous epsilon-optimal value functions when rho is
  Lipschitz. Matches the title and the result. The follow-on claim about when expected information
  gain is Lipschitz is the manuscript's own statement, not attributed to Fehr et al.
- `foster2021dad` (L155, L517): amortized sequential Bayesian experimental design with a learned
  design policy for real-time adaptive experiments. Correct.
- `fountas2020` (L149): deep generative models combined with MCTS for EFE-based planning. Supported.
  "EFE-optimal" is strong wording for an amortized, approximate method, but it is not a clear
  misattribution.
- `friston2010` (L87, L139), `friston2015` (L139), `friston2021` (L146, L185, L218): the
  free-energy principle, the extrinsic and epistemic value decomposition, and sophisticated inference
  as recursive tree search over beliefs. All correct.
- `gneiting2007` (L1688): log and Brier scores as proper scoring rules. Correct.
