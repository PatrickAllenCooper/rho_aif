## Summary verdicts on the four focus items

1. Proposition PI-5 running-average claim. True as stated under conditions (i) to (iv), the projection onto `[0, w_max]`, the update sign `w + a_t (B - U_t)` (matches `dual_update` in `rho_aif/budget.py`), and the schedule `a_t = eta_0 / (1 + delta t)`, gap budgets included. Every step of the Kronecker argument checks (details under defect 5). "Almost surely" is inherited correctly from the a.s. convergence of `w_t`: the Kronecker step is deterministic path by path, so it adds no probabilistic assumption. Two qualifications, both MINOR: the claim needs the specific schedule (through `n a_n -> eta_0/delta > 0`), which the proposition's opening sentence does not pin down, and the a.s. convergence of the projected iterate on a discontinuous regression function is cited to Kushner and Clark, whose projected-algorithm theorem assumes a continuous mean field, although a one-clause argument closes the gap.
2. Notation convention. Consistent in the abstract, both Introductions, the contribution bullet, Definition PI-3, Corollary PI-4, Sections 4.4 and 4.5, Proposition PI-5, the Agents paragraph, the Discussion, the Conclusion, the hero figure and its caption. One occurrence in Section 4.2's corollary paragraph still uses `w^*` for the bracket (defect 1, BLOCKING). The cost-budget section and three producer-generated figures still label brackets or nearest-point markers as `w^*` (defects 2 and 3, MINOR).
3. Definition PI-3 fallback clause. Correct against the solver: `solve_shadow_price_from_curve` sets `bracketed=True` only when a grid weight with `U >= B` follows the last grid weight with `U < B` (`above_after = np.where(us[lo_i + 1:] >= budget)`), so a curve whose final grid point falls back below `B` is flagged unbracketed while the threshold, the supremum of below-budget weights that precede an at-or-above weight, still exists. The later parenthetical "a shape no swept curve exhibits" holds: every row of `results_price_shadow_curves.csv`, `results_price_interleaved_prices.csv`, and `results_price_cost_prices.csv` has `bracketed=True`.

The Table 6 sentence's numbers check against `results_cpomdp_baseline.csv` (`w_star_PIG` = 0.000000, 0.447214, 1.069449, all grid points of `make_log_w_grid(0, 200, 16)`, chosen by nearest point on a 60-episode-per-seed curve, so "lighter" and "nearest grid point rather than a bracket" are accurate), but its clause "takes the same value as at w=1" names a weight the cited CSV does not sample (defect 4, MINOR).

5. MINOR. Line 497 (LNCS 460), Proposition PI-5 statement. Quote: "Its running average does converge to $B$, $\frac{1}{n}\sum_{t<n} U_t \to B$ with probability one, gap budgets included, as the proof below shows." What is wrong: the proof needs `n a_n -> eta_0/delta > 0`, a property of the specific schedule in Equation (`eq:dual_update`), whereas the proposition's first sentence admits any step sizes with `sum a_t = infty` and `sum a_t^2 < infty` and mentions the schedule only parenthetically. Under a schedule with `n a_n -> 0` (for example `a_t = 1/(t log t)`, which satisfies both sums) Kronecker still gives `a_n sum_{t<n} (B - U_t) -> 0` but that no longer implies the `1/n` average converges. Also "Its" grammatically refers to "the expected usage at $w_t$" while the display averages the observed `U_t` (the two averages both converge, the former by the martingale strong law applied to the bounded differences `U_t - U(w_t)`, but only the latter is proved). The Kronecker steps themselves check: `w^*` interior plus `a_t |B - U_t| -> 0` (usage bounded by (ii)) makes the projection inactive from an almost surely finite episode `T` on, so `sum_{t >= T} a_t (B - U_t) = lim w_n - w_T` converges, `1/a_t = (1 + delta t)/eta_0` is positive and increasing to infinity, and `(1/n) sum = (a_n sum) / (n a_n)`. Fix: "The running average of observed usage under the schedule in~\eqref{eq:dual_update} does converge to $B$, $\frac{1}{n}\sum_{t<n} U_t \to B$ with probability one, gap budgets included, as the proof below shows." Optionally, in the proof, replace "because $n\,a_n \to \eta_0/\delta > 0$" with "because $n\,a_n \to \eta_0/\delta > 0$ under the schedule in~\eqref{eq:dual_update}, the step the running-average claim uses".

## Observation, not a defect of this batch

Condition (iii) of Proposition PI-5 uses strict inequalities on both sides of `w^*`. For a usage curve that is a step function, a budget lying on a plateau (nondegenerate level set) violates (iii), so on such a curve the proposition covers gap budgets and isolated-point crossings and not plateau budgets. The new phrase "gap budgets included" is therefore right, and a reader should understand that gap budgets are the main case the proposition covers on the curves this article measures. No change is required. If wanted, one clause after "Condition (iii) does not require $U(w^*) = B$": "and its strict inequalities exclude a budget that the curve attains on a plateau, where the level set is nondegenerate and the recursion has no single point to converge to".
