# Referee report, lens `decision_theory_pomdp`

Manuscript: `paper/full_paper_jair.tex` (2208 lines, PDF 117 pp, Conclusion ends p. 37, References begin p. 38). Code and CSVs at the working tree of 2026-09-28. The tex carried uncommitted edits from another session while this review ran, so line numbers were re-resolved against the working tree at the time of writing and each is paired with a verbatim quote. Scratch outputs for every check below are in `scratch_decision_theory_pomdp/` (read-only scripts, nothing outside this directory was modified).

## 1. Recommendation

Accept with minor revisions.

## 2. Summary

The Price of Information extension is stated and argued correctly. Definition PI-3's four objects (level set, crossing threshold, grid bracket, endpoint mixture) match `crossing_bracket`, `solve_shadow_price_from_curve`, and the mixture probability in `run_budget_frontier.py` line by line. Proposition PI-1's proof is sound and the implementation's relative tie tolerance (`_strictly_better`, `rel * max(|c|, |i|)`, commit favored) is homogeneous of degree one as claimed. Proposition PI-2's two-line argument is correct and the paper is careful not to infer monotone count usage from it. Proposition PI-5's supermartingale proof in Appendix `app:theory_controller` is correct, including the Kronecker step for the running average, and the runner uses exactly the covered variant (`DUAL_W_MAX = 100`, `lr_decay = 0.02`, no iterate above 3.09). The information-floor argument that `w` is not the Lagrange multiplier of the usage cap is correct and is applied consistently in the abstract, Section 6.1, Section 6.8, and Appendix `app:cpomdp_details`. The constrained reference is a genuine cap LP (`lp_mixture`, `highs-ds`, at most two support points asserted), and the same-stream held-out re-evaluation does what the text says, with lineage checks that I reproduced. Twelve of twelve rows of Table `tab:budget_frontier_main` and every number I traced in Sections 6.4 to 6.9 match the CSVs exactly. Two things block an unqualified accept. First, the observe-then-commit POMCP baseline plans against a sign-flipped observation cost: `rho_aif/agents/pomcp.py` adds `+cost` to the simulated return at every observation while the environment and every other planner charge `-cost`. Correcting the sign changes the POMCP rows in Tables `tab:pomcp` and `tab:pomcp-sweep` materially (Bandit POMCP drops from 7.2 observations and 88 percent success to 0.1 observations and 23 percent) and removes two inferences the appendix draws from them. Second, the Diagnosis shortfalls in the same-stream frontier comparison are not reproducible on fresh seeds and contradict the paper's own n=20 TOST for essentially the same policy pair, yet the rare-event qualification the paper gives Tiger is not extended to Diagnosis. Both fixes are local. Neither touches the core equivalence, calibration, or convergence results.

## 3. Strengths

- Every budget object is defined once and used consistently. The level set versus threshold versus bracket versus mixture distinction is maintained through the figures, the tables, and the code, including the closed-enclosure containment statement and the last-crossing convention on Bandit's twice-crossed budget.
- The Lagrangian relationship is handled with unusual care. The two-line information-floor argument (tex 358 to 362) is correct, the mirror-image penalty argument in `app:cpomdp_details` is correct, and the paper never lets `w` drift into being called a multiplier.
- Proposition PI-5 is now proved directly, with the four conditions inside the statement, the gap-budget case handled honestly (weight converges, running usage average converges, expected usage at the iterate need not), and the deployed controller identified as the covered variant. The reset-on-shift heuristic and the mid-run rescale are explicitly excluded from the guarantee.
- The constrained reference is the right object for a cap problem (feasible envelope over sampled points, slack once the cap is not binding) and the same-stream re-evaluation with a lineage check to 1e-9 is a model of how to add a comparator after the fact.
- The frontier study separates "meets expected usage" from "maximizes reward under a cap" and reports the reward cost of spending the budget on Tiger and Bandit rather than hiding it.
- Disclosure discipline is high throughout: retrospective TOST margins, retrospective Tiger tuning result, post hoc LP comparators, the earlier unseeded dual runner and its 2.65 ratio, the once-per-seed versus per-episode seeding distinction.

## 4. Required changes

1. **Appendix `app:pomcp`, tex line 1562, "three fidelity gaps against Silver and Veness must be stated before the numbers."** The observe-then-commit POMCP planner simulates observation cost with the wrong sign. In `rho_aif/agents/pomcp.py`, `_simulate` sets `reward = obs_cost + self.discount * self._simulate(...)` (line 135) and `_rollout` does `total += gamma_power * self.obs_costs[obs_action]` (line 185), with `obs_costs` positive (`[1.0]` on Tiger, `[0.5]*4` on Bandit, from `make_env_config`). The environment charges `-listen_cost` (`tiger.py` line 114), and every other planner subtracts the cost (`planning_infogain.py` `expected_value = -self.obs_costs[k]`, `mcts_efe.py` lines 159 and 308). POMCP therefore plans against a reward in which every observation pays `+c` instead of `-c`, a flat sensing subsidy of `2c` per observation relative to the environment and relative to MCTS-EFE in the same battery. This is a fourth fidelity gap and it is not disclosed. *Evidence*: at the Table `tab:pomcp` protocol (c=10, 1000 simulations, `rollout_depth = H + 3`, per-run planner seed) on 5 seeds x 40 episodes, the code as written reproduces the table (Tiger 1.80 obs, 89.5 percent, -3.35; Diagnosis 3.79, 74.5 percent, -9.09; Bandit 6.95, 90.0 percent, +5.63). With the sign corrected: Tiger 1.56, 88.5 percent, -4.21; Diagnosis 2.61, 61.0 percent, -16.0; Bandit 0.10 obs, 23.0 percent, +3.02. At the sweep's selected Tiger configuration (c=50, uniform, 500 simulations, H=10) success moves from 98.0 to 92.5 +- 2.1 percent and reward from +4.98 to -0.33, and at the selected Diagnosis configuration (c=5, info-gain, 200 simulations) success moves from 72.0 to 56.0 percent (`scratch_decision_theory_pomdp/pomcp_sign_table.csv`, `pomcp_sign_selected.csv`). The headline direction (EFE ahead of POMCP) survives and strengthens, but three statements do not: the Bandit reading "POMCP's Monte Carlo exploration achieves a slightly higher success rate than EFE ... buying that success with 7.20 inspections" (line 1594) and the inference at line 1562 "Bandit ... shows no success gap, so a choice among tests is not by itself what produces one" are artifacts of the subsidy, and the tuned-Tiger fail-to-reject against MCTS-EFE (line 1604, "we fail to reject on either metric") is not established under the correct reward. The compute-matched control, the `results_mcts_efe.csv` POMCP rows, and `select_pomcp_constants.py` inherit the same code path. *Fix*: correct the sign at both sites, add a unit test asserting that a simulated observe-then-commit path returns `-cost + commit reward`, rerun the four producers (`run_pomcp.py`, `run_pomcp_exploration_sweep.py` followed by `select_pomcp_constants.py`, `run_pomcp_compute_matched.py`, the POMCP rows of `run_mcts_experiments.py`), regenerate Tables `tab:pomcp` and `tab:pomcp-sweep`, and rewrite the dependent sentences at lines 1562, 1594, 1596, 1604, 2148, and 2154 against the new CSVs. If a rerun cannot be completed before resubmission, the minimum acceptable change is to state the sign as a fourth fidelity gap with the measured sensitivity above and to withdraw the Bandit inference and the tuned-Tiger fail-to-reject statement. *Placement*: appendix (tables and paragraphs regenerate in place). Main body, net-neutral: the sentence at line 843 "Tuning POMCP's exploration constant recovers most of the initial Tiger gap." is replaced by a sentence of the same length that the rerun supports. Line 750 needs no change unless the retrospective qualification changes.

2. **Section 6.9 `sec:budget_frontier`, tex line 676, "negative $95\%$ intervals exclude zero at nine of twelve attainable budgets, or eight of eleven distinct budgets after the Tiger duplicate is removed."** Two of those eight are the Diagnosis 0.5 and 0.75 rows, and the 0.5 row contradicts the paper's own Section 6.7 evidence without comment. The Diagnosis 0.5 mixture plays w=0.316 with probability 0.94, and on the calibration curve w=0.316 has usage and reward identical to w=1 (the plateau `w in [0.316, 19.3]` at 9.772 and -1.452), while the same-stream reference is 0.91 of the unpenalized SARSOP policy. Up to mixture components worth about +0.04 net, the row compares the w=1 policy with the unpenalized reference, which is the pair the n=20 TOST at line 601 places at +0.146 with paired SE 0.104 (90 percent CI [-0.10, 0.39], 10,000 episodes per arm). The frontier row reports -0.76 +- 0.15 (95 percent CI [-1.19, -0.33]). The two estimates are incompatible at more than three standard errors. *Evidence*: I re-ran the exact pair (Planning+IG w=0.316 against the saved `diagnosis_lam0.policy`) with the frontier study's per-episode seeding. Held-out seeds {7..11} x 100 episodes reproduce the CSV per seed (differences -0.82, -1.00, -1.12, -0.50, -1.60, mean -1.01 +- 0.18). Ten fresh seeds {12..21} x 100 give +0.18 +- 0.25 with per-seed differences from -1.02 to +1.56. The canonical seeds under per-episode seeding give +0.02 +- 0.53 (`scratch_decision_theory_pomdp/diag_check_*.csv`). The held-out shortfall is a seed-set fluctuation in wrong-diagnosis counts (a wrong diagnosis costs 60, the family's implied held-out error rate is 4.2 percent against 2.5 percent canonical, the reference's 2.2 against 3.1), and the df=4 plug-in interval understated it because the five held-out differences happened to be homogeneous (SD 0.40 against 0.79 on fresh seeds). The paper gives exactly this rare-event qualification to Tiger at line 678 and withholds it from Diagnosis, so the count "eight of eleven" reads as more robust than the paper's own data allow. *Fix*: one sentence after the Tiger paragraph at line 678: "Diagnosis carries the same qualification, since a wrong diagnosis costs 60, its 0.5 row compares the w=1 plateau policy with the unpenalized reference that Section 6.7 finds equivalent at n=20, and only Bandit's three shortfalls and Tiger's largest are robust to this check." The fresh-seed pair check, if the authors adopt it, belongs in Appendix `app:frontier_details` as one sentence with the CSV name. *Placement*: main body, net-neutral. Cut the sentence at line 676 "Three intervals include zero, at Diagnosis's smallest and gap targets and Bandit's smallest target." (readable from the table) to fund the new one.

## 5. Optional suggestions

1. **Section 6.5 `sec:pi5`, tex line 456, "The usage at the limit is $U(w^*)$, one side of the jump, so the expected usage at $w_t$ need not converge to $B$."** At a gap budget the iterates approach the threshold from both sides and `U(w_t)` alternates between the two plateau levels along the sequence, which is why the running average can reach `B` while `U(w_t)` does not converge. Saying "takes values on both sides of the jump along the sequence" instead of "is U(w*)" would prevent a reader from inferring `U(w_t) -> U(w*)`. Placement: main body, net-neutral (same clause length).
2. **Appendix `app:dual_details`, tex line 1880 onward.** The restricted recovery time has a floor of 19 by construction (`readaptation_episodes` uses a full 20-episode rolling window with `min_periods=roll`, so the first admissible start index is 19). One sentence stating the floor helps a reader interpret the 51.2 for reset-on-shift, which includes this detection lag. Placement: appendix, one sentence.
3. **Appendix `app:cpomdp_details`, tex lines 1910 to 1925.** The Diagnosis reference support in Table `tab:cpomdp` is lambda=0.05 rather than lambda=0 because SARSOP's lambda=0 solve scores -1.587 at usage 9.707 while lambda=0.05 scores -1.265 at 9.785, so the sampled Lagrangian frontier is not monotone in lambda. A clause noting that this is visible approximate-solver error, and that the envelope LP handles it correctly by selecting over all feasible sampled points, would pre-empt the question. Placement: appendix, one clause.
4. **Table `tab:budget_frontier` panel 3 (`paper/tables/budget_frontier.tex`).** Adding the per-seed SD of the paired difference beside the SE, or the five per-seed differences in the appendix archive description, would let readers see the df=4 fragility that item 2 above turns on. Placement: appendix table, one column or one sentence.
5. **`rho_aif/agents/pomcp.py`.** Independently of item R1, a regression test comparing the planner's simulated return on a fixed observe-then-commit path against `run_otc_episode`'s realized return on the same path would have caught the sign and will catch a recurrence. Placement: code only.
