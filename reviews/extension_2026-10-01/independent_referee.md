# Independent referee review — public-data extension, 2026-10-01

**Recommendation: ACCEPT.** I judge the current manuscript publishable as a bounded methodological and empirical contribution to calibrating an existing information-weighted policy family. Its novelty is modest, and the contribution would be insufficiently supported if sold as a new general acquisition algorithm, a broadly optimal EFE controller, or an operational sensing validation. The final manuscript makes none of those claims. The new negative results strengthen the useful part of the paper: attaining expected usage, obtaining accurate decisions, and transferring calibration are separate questions. No required scientific or editorial revision remains in the version identified below.

This recommendation is independent of earlier simulated referee decisions. I read the current scientific text of both masters, the core proofs and relevant appendices, the frozen sensor-study protocol, the final sensor summary CSV and analysis JSON, and the integrated abstract, sensor section, discussion and conclusion. I compared the masters after normalizing bibliography, checklist, accessibility text and venue-specific figure-width differences: their scientific text matches. I did not rerun the full experiment pipeline or perform a page-by-page visual review; those are separate evidence and layout audits. I inspected the successful build/test logs rather than treating their reported results as my own reruns.

**Contribution and significance.** The strongest contribution is the explicit treatment of an information weight as a calibratable parameter of a selected policy family: population crossing, sampled bracket, episode-level endpoint mixture, and empirical transfer are kept distinct. The experiments show why those distinctions matter, including unattainable sampled targets, inferior same-target mixtures, irrelevant information, inadequate planning horizons and collection shift. The conclusion now states a defensible use condition: an already-selected information-weighted family, a feasible expected-use target, and a transfer check. It also says that the procedure supplies no reason to prefer that family when counting or penalizing accesses meets the need.

The theoretical ingredients are largely established or elementary. Negating a specified reward-minus-information recursion is an algebraic bridge; positive homogeneity gives scale equivariance; endpoint randomization uses linearity; and the controller is scalar stochastic approximation. They should not be presented as independent breakthroughs. The paper earns its contribution through their precise integration and unusually explicit empirical limits. Prior work already connects active inference and POMDP information gathering ([Walraven et al.](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10458-024-09683-4)), develops broader EFE correspondences ([Sweeney et al.](https://www.mdpi.com/1099-4300/28/1/1)), and formulates an information constraint within the Bethe treatment of EFE ([Kouw](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17167)). The current positioning acknowledges this context. Greedy conditional-information acquisition is also established ([Covert et al.](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v202/covert23a.html)), and nongreedy acquisition has an extensive modern comparator ([Valancius et al.](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/valancius24a.html)). The new study is appropriately not sold as state-of-the-art AFA benchmarking. A neural-method benchmark is not required for the claim actually made.

**Proof and definition consistency.** I found no unresolved proof defect. The EFE result is explicitly conditional on the reward-based recursion, matched information units, horizon and tie-breaking; the variable-length preference normalizer is not silently discarded. The bit/nat distinction is clear. State preservation is a sufficient hypothesis for the stated factored reduction, and the destructive-sensing example is not claimed to refute transition-aware EFE. The diagnostic is not promoted to an error bound.

The scale proof correctly applies to the closed-loop receding-horizon policy and distinguishes count from rescaled cost usage. The comparative-statics result establishes monotonic information, not monotonic sensor count. The two-state onset and second-observation thresholds remain restricted to their respective horizons; the multi-state reductions are labelled heuristic. The controller proof is sound: the projected squared-error supermartingale handles discontinuous usage, separation away from the crossing forces the limiting error to zero, and eventual inactivity of projection plus Kronecker's lemma yields ordinary-average usage for the specified harmonic schedule. It supplies no guarantee for arbitrary nonmonotone curves or the reset-on-shift variant.

Definition PI-3 is materially clearer after the four-object revision. Its finite-final-crossing scope is explicit, the containment statement concerns the closed enclosure, and the no-missed-crossing condition includes crossings beyond the grid. The empirical mixture uses estimated usage, so equality on calibration does not become a population guarantee. The closure, interior and endpoint-attainment statements are now distinct. The descending-only example and non-straddling fallback remain. Describing the related constrained-POMDP controller as a related feedback structure, rather than the same dual-gradient mechanism, also preserves the distinction between the information weight and a usage-cap multiplier.

**What the public-data extension establishes.** The new study addresses the earlier absence of real recorded observations, learned likelihoods and targets chosen independently of usage curves. It preserves the decisive methodological features: actual held-out sensor records rather than draws from the fitted model, training-only quantization and likelihood fitting, fixed targets 2/4/8, matched model and depth for the direct comparator, whole-case mixture selection, paired uncertainty with calibration reselection, and later chronological batches kept separate.

The results do not turn into a success-only story. The two-access request is below the frozen sampled family's range; this is not a theorem about every untested weight. Four- and eight-access mixtures use 4.006 and 8.019 accesses on represented-period test cases, with the reported simultaneous-family conditional intervals inside the declared half-access margin. The all-three-target criterion still fails. Accuracy is lower than direct equality and fixed-count greedy-information policies, and even the best sampled equality mixtures within the information family do better. Realized comparator usage is reported, so a fitted equality mixture is not falsely called exactly matched on test cases. Later batches exceed the target materially, and accuracy degrades sharply. One approximate fitted model and one collection archive cannot establish a general robustness effect or distinguish the causal roles of class composition and sensor-response shift; the manuscript says so.

The extension therefore closes a meaningful evidence gap without closing the separate gap of an operator-supplied requirement or a deployment study. It does not test deeper learned-model planning, destructive sensing, new instruments, or repeated retraining. These are correctly bounded limitations rather than missing experiments necessary for the present contribution. I would not demand another dataset simply to rescue the unfavorable comparisons.

**Clarity and length.** The source is clearer and the main argument is shorter despite the added study. The finalized build has 108 JAIR pages with references beginning on page 33, compared with 111 pages and references on page 38 before this extension/concision work; the LNCS version has 136 pages. The complete appendix archive remains long, but the reader no longer needs to traverse it to understand the argument or the strongest negative evidence. The four-part definition, shorter abstract and explicit final use condition are substantive improvements. Further appendix compression would be optional editorial work, not an acceptance condition, and should not displace the failure disclosures or proof assumptions. The successful build log and 540-test report provide supporting verification; this review does not certify the isolated Overleaf package, which is being checked separately.
