# High-level audit before appendix consolidation

Baseline: `c5125ab`, preserved in `paper/legacy/2026-10-02_pre_appendix_consolidation/` before any manuscript edit. The archived version has 108 JAIR pages. References begin on page 33 and Appendix A begins on page 36. The appendices and checklist occupy the remainder through page 108. The LNCS companion has 136 pages.

The scientific contribution is already deliberately bounded: calibrating expected usage within a specified information-weighted policy family. Its theoretical assumptions, the distinction between an equality target and a usage cap, the non-optimality of selected mixtures, and the adverse real-data transfer findings are central. Those limits must remain visible. A page reduction should remove repeated presentation, not make the result appear stronger.

The appendix structure mixes two layers accumulated at different stages. Early appendices give per-environment tables, robustness batteries and implementation details. Later appendices reproduce expanded discussions of many of those same experiments after the main body was shortened. The main opportunity is to give each protocol, result and qualification one clear home, with cross-references elsewhere. Shortening proofs indiscriminately or hiding negative studies would damage the paper more than retaining a few extra pages.

Three independent proposal agents cover early empirical material, later empirical material and theory. Their remit is guarded source replacements, not edits to the live masters. The root separately audits the reproducibility checklist. The largest candidates are repeated budget/reference interpretations, duplicate core result rows, long experiment descriptions already specified in environment tables, and illustrative appendix plots whose distinctive conclusions can be stated briefly. The adaptive-test decomposition and destructive-sensing example retain explanatory roles. Full data, producers and original figures remain in the repository even when a redundant rendering leaves the manuscript.

All proofs must remain independently checkable. In particular, the variable-length normalizer, reward/information units, finite-grid crossing conditions, projected controller assumptions and proof, horizon limits and destructive-sensing boundary remain explicit. Empirical compression must retain calibration versus held-out selection, cap versus target references, conditional uncertainty, tuning chronology, fixed internal planner seeds, failed targets and baseline limitations.

The baseline contains 26 appendix sections. Float barriers contain experiments within sections but are not unconditional page breaks. The first pass therefore leaves font sizes, margins and float parameters unchanged. Genuine content cuts will be compiled before considering any structural layout change. Page savings are measured from rendered PDFs, not inferred from source word counts.

Final acceptance requires mirrored scientific text in both masters, resolved cross-references, independent applied-change audits, unchanged evidence archives and legacy hashes, legible rendered pages, and an independently compiled Overleaf package. The requested 20–30-page reduction is a target within those constraints, not a reason to omit necessary evidence.
