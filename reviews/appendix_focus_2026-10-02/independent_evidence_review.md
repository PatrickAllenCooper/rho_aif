The cuts do not require restoration of the removed full result tables, weight atlas, per-test trace, proper-score comparison, pymdp check, zero-shot transfer table, or random-instance near-optimality survey. The corresponding specific claims and promises have been withdrawn or replaced with claims supported by the retained evidence. Repeating their complete numerical tables in the appendix would add length without supplying a missing premise.

The remaining evidence still makes the main adverse findings visible:

- The expected-usage target is distinct from a reward-maximizing cap. The endpoint mixture need not be the best equal-usage mixture, and the Bandit exception remains numerical rather than merely acknowledged.
- Calibration-derived simulated targets are disclosed as such. The three unattainable targets, held-out overspend, fitted-reference selection, conditional plug-in intervals, omitted multiplicity and feasibility uncertainty, Tiger's rare-event caveat, and the non-blind Diagnosis replication pair all remain.

Retrospective access, researcher-defined costs/targets, one fitted approximate model, and conditional bootstrap scope remain explicit.
- The weight-one anchor still loses on Testbed and Tileworld, fails to improve the largest RockSample instance, and does not show an EFE-specific sensor-mismatch benefit. Posterior-vote's Bandit non-separation remains.
- POMCP configuration sensitivity, the stronger standalone RockSample heuristic, the shared leaf's phantom exit cost, the unsuccessful deeper large-instance search, the MCTS component ablation's non-rejections, and coupled architectural features remain visible. The text does not attribute all solver differences to information gain.
- Reward-irrelevant sensing and destructive sensing retain their concrete failure modes and scope restrictions. The zero-distractor result is not presented as a general learned-relevance result.

This is an adequate claim-focused appendix. The surviving protocols are readable, and the data/producer pointers supply the full numerical records without requiring them all to be printed.
