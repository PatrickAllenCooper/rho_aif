# Claim-focused appendix audit

The preceding pass removed repetition within sections but retained most of the accumulated study roster. JAIR still has 42 pages of scientific appendices. The published-volume measurement (mean 11.29 and median 13 pages among papers with appendices) is a useful comparison, not a hard ceiling. The next pass changes what the paper includes rather than shrinking every paragraph.

The essential chain is: the specified EFE recursion identifies a member of the information-weighted family; calibration targets expected usage rather than a cap; grid crossings and mixtures have restricted guarantees; scaling and stationary control have explicit assumptions; held-out synthetic comparisons and learned public-sensor evaluation reveal the attainable usage and its reward/transfer costs. Evidence that changes the interpretation of those claims must remain.

Retain independently checkable proofs and counterexamples, environment and planner definitions, statistical families and seed exceptions, reference construction/selection qualifications, within-family failures, and the learned-sensor protocol. Keep adverse results that rule out broad superiority, arbitrary-depth conclusions, model robustness or deployment claims. The main paper already reports the principal result tables and failure cases.

Candidates for removal are duplicate appendix tables/plots of those same results, extended readings of every row, illustrative individual trajectories, catalogs of operating points, exploratory random-horizon grids, transfer experiments that add no distinct conclusion, and heuristic cross-domain extrapolations of the two-state calculation. Their original data, code, figures and generated tables remain in the repository and frozen version. Removing a peripheral experiment also requires removing its dependent claim or promise from the main prose/checklist, rather than pointing to absent evidence.

Three disjoint proposal reviews cover the early empirical appendices, theory/protocols, and late result interpretations. The tentative combined allocation is roughly 15–20 scientific appendix pages; actual clear and sufficient evidence governs the final size. The required submission checklist is tracked separately and retains its questions and honest verdicts. Every main-text repair is guarded and recorded. No font reduction, margin change, compressed table text or unreviewed external supplement is used to reach the target.

Fresh independent final reviewers must assess the resulting whole paper and the retained evidence without inheriting previous ACCEPT verdicts. Required corrections take precedence over the page target. The final package will compile from its own source dependencies, and the edited pages will be inspected visually.
